### [Nvidia CMP 170HX Mining Card Cracked to Unlock 80GB VRAM, Prices Surge](https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/roll/2026-08-03/doc-inikzqsf4659769.shtml) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The card, released in 2021, uses the same GA100 die as the A100 but was heavily firmware-limited for cryptocurrency mining. telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 3, 11:29

**「Background」** The CMP 170HX was Nvidia&\#x27;s dedicated cryptocurrency mining card, built on the same GA100 GPU as the data-center A100 but artificially restricted through one-time-programmable \(OTP\) fuses that limited compute, memory capacity, and PCIe connectivity. These hardware fuses were considered irreversible, which made the card nearly useless after mining and drove its price down to e-waste levels. The exploit instead targets a vulnerability in the Falcon security coprocessor&\#x27;s DMA handling, allowing researchers to overwrite registers and disable the fuses in software.

**「Impact」** The exploit transforms a cheap, locked mining card into an affordable 80 GB VRAM option for AI inference, causing used prices to spike dramatically; however, long-term stability and the maximum unlockable capacity for different card batches remain unverified.
